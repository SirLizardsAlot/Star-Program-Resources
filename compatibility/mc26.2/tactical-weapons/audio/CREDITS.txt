STAR PROGRAM tactical weapon audio

Firearm recordings by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney.
The Free Firearm Sound Library. CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

Shared magazine reload by SpringySpringo, recorded with an airsoft gun. CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds

Dry Fire by brianhanson2nd, authentic S&W 9mm trigger click. CC0 1.0.
Public HQ preview used, with downloaded MP3 and decoded source WAV preserved.
https://freesound.org/people/brianhanson2nd/sounds/171211/

Minigun fire is a designed per-round report using an AK-47 7.62mm recording.
It is not an authentic rotary-gun recording or a baked firing loop.
Shared distant fire uses an actual mid-distance AR-15 recording, additionally
filtered for high-frequency air loss. No far-distance field recording is claimed.
